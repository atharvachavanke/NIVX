The Problem

India's Broken Financial Landscape

Millions of Indians want to build wealth. The system makes it unnecessarily hard. Here's why.

Current State

7 Critical Problems Holding India Back

01

Fragmented Ecosystem

The average Indian investor uses 6–8 separate apps — Zerodha, INDmoney, CAMS, NPS portal, bank apps. Each app has different data, different interfaces, and no unified picture exists.

02

Crisis of Trust

From WhatsApp investment scams to fraudulent advisors, Indians lose thousands of crores annually to financial fraud. There's no universal verification layer for investment claims.

03

Poor Financial Education

Less than 27% of Indians are financially literate. The education that exists is scattered, generic, and disconnected from real investment decisions.

04

Complex Investing

Investing in equities, mutual funds, bonds, REITs, and alternatives each require separate accounts, separate KYC, and entirely separate mental models.

05

Opportunity Blindness

Millions of quality investment opportunities go undiscovered because there's no intelligent discovery layer. Most Indians invest in only what they've heard of through word of mouth.

06

Information Overload

Between business news, research reports, broker recommendations, and social media tips — Indians are drowning in unfiltered, often contradictory financial information.

07

Security Vulnerabilities

Critical financial documents — demat account details, insurance policies, property papers — are stored in email inboxes, WhatsApp chats, or paper. Exposed to theft and loss.

Proof Mode

Trust Shouldn't Require Faith

Proof Mode is NIVX's flagship verification system — the first of its kind in Indian fintech. Before you invest a single rupee, every material claim about an opportunity is independently verified against authoritative sources.

  • Regulatory registration verification (SEBI, RBI, IRDAI)
  • Historical returns validation against audited records
  • Advisor credential authentication
  • Company incorporation and legal standing checks
  • Red flag alerts for suspicious patterns
  • Transparent audit trail for every verification
